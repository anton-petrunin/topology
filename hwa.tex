\section*{Final exam:}
\label{Final}\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Final exam}

Wed, May 6, 12:20--14:10; McAllister 106 (\sout{Thomas Bldg 119}).
\textbf{Note that the room was changed.}

\

It will be an oral exam.
You will have one hour to prepare a mini-presentation on one random topic (ticket) from the following list.
The examiner may interrupt your presentation and ask additional questions.
If your answer is satisfactory, you will be given an additional problem and extra time to solve it for perfect score.

As ususal, you may prepare and use a cheat sheet --- one-sided standard paper size, include diagrams and formulas, but at most 20 words.
Do not make it cryptic.

\

\textbf{The following list is preliminary.}

\

\begin{enumerate}
\item Metric spaces: definitions, balls, open and closed sets, open balls are open, equivalence of the definitions of continuity.

\item Topological spaces: definitions, examples, and continuity; composition of continuous maps is continuous.

\item Interior, closure, and boundary, with examples; closure of the closure, boundary is closed.

\item Subspaces and the induced topology.

\item Homeomorphisms, embeddings, open maps, and closed maps (examples and counterexamples).

\item Bases, prebases, their relations, and examples.

\item Product topology and quotient spaces.

\item Continuous group actions and orbit spaces.

\item Compactness: definition and the finite intersection property.

\item The image of a compact set is compact.

\item Closed subsets of a compact set are compact.

\item Compactness of a closed interval.

\item Compactness of a product space.

\item Compactness of a metric space $\Rightarrow$ sequential compactness.

\item Lebesgue number.

\item Completeness of compact metric spaces.

\item Hausdorff spaces: definitions, basic properties, and relation to metric spaces.

\item Compact subsets of Hausdorff spaces are closed.

\item Maps from compact spaces to Hausdorff spaces, and an application to quotient topology.

\item Baire's theorem and G-delta sets, with an example of an application.

\item Connectedness: definitions and unions.

\item Images of connected sets are connected.

\item Sets squeezed between a connected set and its closure are connected.

\item Connected components, cut points, and an application.

\item Connectedness of a closed interval.

\item Path-connectedness $\Rightarrow$ connectedness. The flea and comb, or a similar example (without proof).

\item Homotopy: definitions, examples, operations, and homotopy classes.

\item Retracts and deformation retracts: definitions; retracts of Hausdorff spaces are closed.

\item Contractible spaces, homotopy type, and their relations.

\item Retracts of contractible spaces are contractible.

\item Paths, homotopy of paths, and its properties.

\item Fundamental group: definition and group structure.

\item Simply connected spaces; the sphere is simply connected.

\item Induced homomorphism: definitions and main properties.

\item Inner automorphisms and change of base point.

\item Computation of $\pi_1\,S^1$, degree, and an application.

\item Fundamental theorem of algebra.

\item Covering spaces: definitions; injectivity of the induced homomorphism.

\item Normal coverings, freely discontinuous groups, and finite free actions.

\item Real projective plane, the Borsuk--Ulam theorem, and an application.

\item Klein bottle: definition and noncommutativity of its fundamental group.
\end{enumerate}
